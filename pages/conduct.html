Code of Conduct

Home/Conduct

NBA Lokoja Branch expects respectful, professional conduct in all interactions on this platform.

Be respectful

Treat all users with dignity and professionalism. Harassment and hate speech are not tolerated.

Protect privacy

Do not share confidential or personal information without authorization.

Use official channels

For official complaints or disciplinary matters, use the branch’s established procedures.

Reporting

Concerns can be reported to the Secretariat at secretariat@nbalokoja.org.
