Stay Sharp. Stay Compliant.

NBA Lokoja Branch is a fully accredited centre under the NBA Institute of Continuing Legal Education (ICLE). We deliver mandatory and elective CPD/CLE programmes designed to keep our members at the cutting edge of legal practice and ethics.

Members earn credit hours by participating in approved branch programmes; transcripts are tracked through the member portal and reflected in the national NBA-ICLE record.
