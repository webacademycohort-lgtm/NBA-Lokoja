Areas of Practice

The Diverse Practice of Our Members

Members of NBA Lokoja Branch practise in virtually every area of contemporary Nigerian law.

Civil & Commercial Litigation

Dispute resolution before all courts of record — from magistrate to apex court — across civil and commercial matters.

Constitutional & Public Law

Public interest, fundamental rights enforcement, election petitions and judicial review of executive action.

Corporate & Commercial

Company law, M&A advisory, secretarial services, tax planning and regulatory compliance.

Property & Real Estate

Conveyancing, perfection of title, land use, tenancy and estate management across Kogi State.

Family & Probate

Matrimonial causes, child custody, inheritance, administration of estates and adoption.

Criminal Defence & Prosecution

Defence and prosecution of criminal matters, plea bargaining and post-conviction relief.

Energy & Natural Resources

Oil & gas, solid minerals, power and other extractive-sector legal services.

Labour & Employment

Industrial relations, employment contracts, NICN practice and workplace investigations.

Human Rights & Pro Bono

Public interest litigation, civil liberties, and access-to-justice programmes for indigent persons.

Tax & Revenue Practice

Tax advisory, dispute resolution before the Tax Appeal Tribunal and revenue administration.

Alternative Dispute Resolution

Mediation, arbitration and negotiation services for commercial and civil disputes.

Academic & Teaching

Members in scholarship and teaching at universities and the Nigerian Law School.
