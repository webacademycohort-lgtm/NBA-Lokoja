A Word from the Chairman

Bar–Bench Synergy is Key

SA

Sumaila A. Abbas, Esq.

Chairman, NBA Lokoja Branch · 2025–2027

"My fellow members of the Confluence Bar, it is an immense privilege to serve as your Chairman during this tenure. Our branch stands at the meeting point of two great rivers — and so too must our work flow with the combined strength of advocacy and stewardship."

Under our leadership, the branch will pursue an agenda of stronger bar–bench collaboration, expanded continuing legal education, robust welfare for our members, and bold advocacy on matters of public interest in Kogi State.

We invite every member — silk and stuff alike — to join hands with the Executive in building a branch worthy of the legacy we have inherited.
