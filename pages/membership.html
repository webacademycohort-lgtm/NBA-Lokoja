Why Join

The Confluence Bar Advantage

Membership is open to all lawyers called to the Nigerian Bar with a place of practice or residence in Kogi State.

Continuing Education

Free or discounted CLE programmes that count toward mandatory CPD requirements.

Welfare & Support

Member welfare scheme, bereavement support, and targeted assistance in times of need.

Collective Voice

A unified, principled voice on matters affecting the bar, the bench and the public.

Resource Library

Access to the members-only digital library — practice notes, precedents and journals.

Digital ID Card

Secure digital identification card and downloadable annual membership certificate.

Networking

Annual Law Week, dinners, forums and committees — connect with leaders of the bar.
