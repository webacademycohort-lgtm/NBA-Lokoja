Welcome Message

A Branch Rooted in Integrity, Excellence & Service

The Lokoja Branch of the Nigerian Bar Association — affectionately known as the Confluence Bar — sits at the historic meeting point of the Niger and Benue rivers. Since its inception, the branch has nurtured generations of lawyers committed to advancing justice, defending fundamental rights, and upholding the dignity of the legal profession in Kogi State.

Through dedicated continuing legal education, robust bar–bench engagement, welfare programmes, and impactful public advocacy, NBA Lokoja remains a leading voice in the Nigerian legal community.

Pillars of Our Branch

Four enduring commitments shape every initiative of the Confluence Bar.

Rule of Law

Defending constitutional democracy, judicial independence, and equal access to justice.

Legal Education

Mandatory CLE programmes that keep our members at the cutting edge of legal practice.

Member Welfare

Solidarity, support and professional development for every member of our branch.

Public Service

Pro bono representation, civic education, and policy advocacy for the people of Kogi.
