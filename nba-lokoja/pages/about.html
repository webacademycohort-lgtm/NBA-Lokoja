Our Story

Where the Niger Meets the Benue, the Bar Stands United

The Lokoja Branch of the Nigerian Bar Association — popularly called the Confluence Bar — has, for over four decades, been the voice and shield of legal practitioners in Kogi State. Our members serve in courts ranging from the customary tribunals of the rural areas to the State High Court and Federal High Court divisions in Lokoja.

From an initial cluster of practitioners called to the bar in the early years of statehood, the branch has grown into one of the most active and respected branches of the NBA, with a vibrant membership of senior advocates, mid-career counsel, young wigs, in-house lawyers, and academics.

Today, NBA Lokoja stands as a community of professionals committed to integrity, excellence, courage and professionalism — the four enduring core values of the Nigerian Bar Association.

Mission

To use the law as an instrument of social change and to defend the integrity, independence and welfare of the bar in Kogi State.

Vision

To rank among the foremost branches of the Nigerian Bar Association — exemplary in service, scholarship and stewardship.

Core Values

Integrity · Excellence · Courage · Professionalism — the cornerstones of every action we take as a branch.

Our Objectives

What We Set Out to Achieve

1. Defend the Independence of the Bar

Maintain and defend the integrity and independence of the Bar and the Judiciary in Kogi State.

2. Promote Access to Justice

Champion timely and affordable access to justice for all citizens, regardless of background or means.

3. Advance Legal Education

Promote and support continuing legal education, scholarship and the production of legal materials.

4. Member Welfare & Discipline

Look after the welfare and discipline of members, ensuring the highest professional standards.

5. Community Engagement

Engage with the public through pro bono work, civic education, and policy advocacy.

6. Bar–Bench Synergy

Foster respectful and productive relationships with the judiciary and other stakeholders.
