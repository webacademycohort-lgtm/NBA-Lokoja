Membership

Who is eligible to join NBA Lokoja Branch?

Any lawyer called to the Nigerian Bar whose primary place of practice or residence is in Kogi State is eligible. New wigs are warmly welcomed.

How do I apply for membership?

Visit our Membership page and complete the online application form. The membership committee reviews submissions within five working days.

How much are the annual dues?

Junior members (under 7 years post-call) pay ₦15,000; senior members pay ₦25,000; SANs pay ₦50,000 — all per annum.

How can I pay my dues?

Once logged in to the member portal, you can pay securely online via card, bank transfer or USSD.

Continuing Legal Education

Are CLE programmes free for members?

Most flagship CLE programmes are free for financial members. Specialised masterclasses may attract a nominal fee.

How do I get my CLE transcript?

Login to your member portal and navigate to the CLE section. Your transcript is auto-generated and downloadable.

Member Portal

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Click Forgot Password on the login page. A reset link will be emailed to you immediately.

Can I download my membership certificate?

Yes. Once your dues are confirmed for the year, your membership certificate becomes available for download from the dashboard.

How do I update my profile?

From the dashboard, navigate to Profile. Update your details and click save — changes apply immediately.

Welfare & Support

What support does the welfare scheme provide?

The scheme supports members during bereavement, serious illness and other emergencies, in line with the welfare committee's policies.

How do I request welfare assistance?

Submit a confidential request through the member portal under Support / Help Desk, or contact the Welfare Secretary directly.
